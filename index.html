About

BUKFEST 2025 is Bukidnon's premier cultural and business summit, celebrating the rich heritage and entrepreneurial spirit of the region through exhibitions, workshops,
and performances.

Spotlight Local

Bring awareness and exposure to various local businesses and organizations by giving them an avenue to showcase and sell their products/services

Connect & Collaborate

Create an environment where people can seek fruitful collaboration with other locals, suppliers, and partners

Community Empowerment

Equip business owners and people from different communities with the means to enhance their individual practices by bringing new knowledge & ideas
